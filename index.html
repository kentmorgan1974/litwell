Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) · Mac with Apple silicon or Intel · free · nothing else to install

What it does

  • Searches PubMed, Crossref, OpenAlex, Europe PMC and arXiv for papers.
  • Searches preprint servers such as bioRxiv, SSRN, OSF and ChemRxiv.
  • Searches US and worldwide patents (needs a free key, explained in the app's help).
  • Saves titles, authors, journals, dates, DOIs and working links to one Excel file (or CSV, text, Markdown, RIS, BibTeX), skipping duplicates.

First time you open it

Litwell isn't signed with a paid developer certificate, so your computer asks you to confirm once. It's a one-time step.

Windows
  1. Click Download for Windows, then open the file.
  2. If you see “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
Mac
  1. Click Download for Mac and double-click the zip to unpack it.
  2. Drag Litwell to Applications and open it.
  3. If macOS says it can't check the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. (On older macOS: right-click the app, choose Open.)

Your privacy: searches go straight from your computer to the public databases. Results and settings stay on your computer. There's no account and no tracking.
